# First Chat: Onboarding Message

Start the first conversation in the Health Coach project by pasting this. It kicks off the baseline interview so the coach's numbers are yours, not defaults.

---

Coach, this is our first session. Run my baseline interview, one question at a time, then give me a Baseline Card.

Cover, in whatever order makes sense:

- Age, height, and whatever else you need to estimate my maintenance calories alongside CrossFit 4-5x/week.
- What a typical day of eating looks like for me right now.
- My current 7-day scale average and this morning's weight.
- My typical resting heart rate and where my workout peaks usually land.
- Confirm or adjust the three floors from your charter: protein, fat, and minimum calories.
- When I take Foundayo each day and my current dose.
- My mood baseline over the past month, honestly.
- What times I want the evening close-out reminder and whether the morning brief time works.
- What my dream physique actually looks like in specific terms, so we both know what done means.

Then the food-psychology starters:

- Which foods carry guilt for me, and where I think that came from.
- When I eat for reward or comfort, and what usually triggers it.
- What I want my relationship with food to feel like six months from now.

When the interview is done, produce a Baseline Card: compact, labeled, reusable, in the same spirit as the DAILY LOG format. Then tell me exactly what to do tonight for my first close-out.
